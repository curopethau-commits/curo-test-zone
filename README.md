# curo-test-zone

## Doodle Garden (WIP)

Prototype of the AR plant game: kids draw a plant, it turns into 3D, they plant its seed on a map, water it to make it grow, and can view it in AR on a phone.

Play: https://curopethau-commits.github.io/curo-test-zone/doodle-garden/

Status: **WIP**. Plants are saved on the device only in this version (no shared server yet).

## Bethan's Forest Disco (WIP)

Bethan (and Bunny's) super duper 8th birthday forest disco: guests scan the QR on the big screen, draw a dancer on their phone and flick it onto the dance floor. Branched from Will's Intergalactic Tarranium.

Big screen: https://curopethau-commits.github.io/curo-test-zone/forest-disco/
Phones (via the QR): https://curopethau-commits.github.io/curo-test-zone/forest-disco/draw.html

Status: **WIP**. Needs the `forest_disco` database rule (see forest-disco/README.md) and the forest illustration (`forest-disco/forest.png`).

## Keep Level Test

After Effects expression demo: a square parented to a spinning null stays level while it scales up and moves to the top.

View: https://curopethau-commits.github.io/curo-test-zone/keep-level/
