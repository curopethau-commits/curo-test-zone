Pick a voice above, then draw. Higher is higher pitch, faster is louder. Up to 4 fingers at once.
You sound like a…

Every bird has a song that is theirs alone.

Draw lines in the dark and they become birdsong. Height sets the pitch, speed sets the volume, and each of the four lights sings with a different voice, the way some birds sing several notes at once.

Watch your song appear as a spectrogram at the bottom. That picture is how scientists tell one bird from another.

Let’s sing! Do you want to freestyle, or mimic a bird?
