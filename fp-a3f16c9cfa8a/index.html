Every bird has a song that is theirs alone.

Draw lines in the dark and they become birdsong. Height sets the pitch, speed sets the volume, and the way you move picks the voice: smooth lines whistle, wobbles trill, flicks chirp and scribbles buzz.

Watch your song appear as a spectrogram at the bottom. That picture is how scientists tell one bird from another.
