This is a sample trip so you can see how it works. Tap ✦ on any day for more things to do there, from GetYourGuide. Your plans save in this browser.

Stops

Use − / + to shorten or extend a stop. Everything after it slides along.

Drag a coloured place band to move where that stop starts: drag the first stop to move the whole trip, drag a later one back or forward to shorten or stretch the stop before it. Click a trip day to change what's on, the travel or where you sleep. Click an empty day to start a new trip on it. Tap ✦ on a day for more things to do there. The .ics file holds every trip.

On a phone: use − / + in Stops to change nights. To drag a band, press and hold it until it's outlined, then slide your finger up or down; the page scrolls when you reach the top or bottom edge.
